About

I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of California, Merced, working in the Inclusive Interaction Lab under Dr. Ahmed Arif. My research sits at the intersection of human-computer interaction, virtual reality, and accessibility — designing input techniques that work for the way people actually move, look, and gesture.

Before UC Merced, I completed a B.Sc. Honors in Computer Science (First Class) at the National University of Science and Technology, Zimbabwe. Outside the lab, I build trading systems and education under my brand GCT, and DJ and produce music.
